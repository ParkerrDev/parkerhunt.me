I’m a cofounder of **Kite Industries, Inc.**, where I lead software and hardware
development. We’re building a tabletop printer for PCBs and circuit boards.

Our goal is to help people iterate on electronics more quickly, with the ability
to make circuit boards where they work. We’re talking with engineers and
hardware teams about their prototyping workflows, what slows them down, and
what they need from a printer.

[Visit Kite](https://kiteprinters.com) for company updates or to get in touch.
