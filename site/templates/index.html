About

I am a student in Clovis, California. I build software that is fast, small and finished — sites that load in one request, tools that do one thing, systems I can explain end to end.

Away from a screen I have been doing martial arts for twelve years and hold a first-degree adult black belt. I have hiked to the summit of Half Dome at six, El Capitan at eight, and Mt. Whitney — the tallest mountain in the contiguous United States, 14,505 ft — at thirteen, in a thunderstorm.

Linux and macOS daily. Not Windows, unless the work insists.
