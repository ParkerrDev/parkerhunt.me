How this was made

Every case on the shelf was read out and typed as read, one line each, then matched against TMDb. Where a title exists more than once the earliest release wins, unless the line itself says otherwise: a year, the word original, or an actor's name all outrank that rule, which is how walking tall (dwayne johnson) lands on 2004 and plain walking tall lands on 1973.

{{ dad.films }} films and {{ dad.tv }} television series were identified out of {{ dad.distinct }} lines, from {{ dad.copies_total }} discs. Posters are from TMDb and are served from TMDb's own CDN rather than re-hosted, which is their condition for licensing the artwork.
